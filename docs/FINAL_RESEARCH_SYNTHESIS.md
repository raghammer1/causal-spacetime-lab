# Final research synthesis: seven experiments on causal and quantum structure

**Overall conclusion B — useful mathematical/computational research, but insufficient
evidence to pursue the physical hypothesis through more simulations.** The seventh
and final experiment gives NO-GO for its registered finite unconditioned locality
benchmark: every parameter/size cell has 0/12 joint successes, with simultaneous
exact upper bound 0.402190 against the required 0.8. Independent audits reproduce
that decision. Continuum scaling and broader relational protocols remain unresolved.

This closes the current computational programme. No eighth experiment, replacement
Hamiltonian or further classifier optimization is proposed. The distinction
throughout is between a theorem under assumptions, a reproducible numerical
observation, and a statement about physical spacetime.

## The question behind the water analogy

Water suggests that a smooth macroscopic description can arise from microscopic
constituents with different properties. This is a motivation for a research
question, not evidence that spacetime has analogous constituents. Molecules and
hydrodynamic limits have independent empirical and theoretical support. In this
laboratory the causal events, qubit factorization, laws, states and simulation
clock are assumptions. A graph that resembles a surface does not supply the
missing physical principle or controlled limit.

The programme progressively separated five questions: what order retains about
a supplied geometry; what selected summaries discard; whether intrinsic growth
reproduces a geometric law; what fixed quantum correlations retain about supplied
couplings; and whether quantum links themselves organize operational locality.

## Mathematical results that changed the interpretation

**Information can be absent, rather than merely difficult to extract.** Two
independent random rankings have exactly the same full-order distribution as
uniform d=2 diamond sampling. Even an observer with the entire order cannot infer
which provenance generated it. Different finite orders share ordering fraction,
height and the full interval-cardinality histogram. Experiment 4 separates some
of them with induced 2+2, interval connectivity and overlap information, while
the explicitly postregistration C12 versus two C6 height-two examples share the entire selected enhanced vector.
A counting argument extends bounded-summary collisions on unrestricted orders.
These are limits on inference from stated observables, not assertions that all
restricted geometric classes are unidentifiable.

**Some apparent extra features are old information.** The three-chain count is
Σ_m m a_m, and degree covariance follows from that count and the relation number.
Motif correlations are nonzero even in the supplied geometric law. Treating more
features as independent evidence is mathematically unjustified without analysis.

**Local order laws can improve a conditional reconstruction problem.** In an iid
uniform sample from a causally convex flat region, conditioning on related endpoints
and the interior count gives the corresponding diamond law. This explains why
local dimension estimates can recover d=2/3/4 in slabs while global ordering-fraction
estimates shift with boundary. It requires the stated volume law and causal convexity;
clipped boxes can violate the premise. Matching that law does not certify a global
embedding, and small order cardinality does not necessarily mean a small coordinate
neighborhood in a Lorentzian setting.

**Growth laws have structural limits.** Fixed-p transitive percolation has a dense
chain-sampling limit under the stated assumptions, while persistent sparse
incomparabilities keep the longest chain from spanning almost all events.
Published ordinary-CSG almost-semiorder limits exclude the nonzero 2+2 density of
nondegenerate flat diamonds in that specified convergence sense. A height-two
control can nevertheless match the selected 2+2/thinning benchmark. Uniform
thinning preserves every parent's motif density in conditional expectation; that
identity is not a geometric signature.

**Quantum information does not automatically specify a metric.** Local sign gauges
can change Ising coupling signs without changing MI/negativity. Bell and purified
AND examples violate proposed information-distance axioms. GHZ has pairwise MI
without pairwise entanglement; Bell products can satisfy an additive cut relation
while disconnected. Shortest-path closure enforces triangle inequalities by construction; it can
remain a pseudometric until zero-distance points are quotiented. Participation
rank and arbitrary truncated layouts are not spacetime dimensions.

**Symmetry and operational influence constrain quantum-link interpretations.**
For the final model, finite symmetric preparations retain uniform labeled link
expectations and equal target responses. This leaves relational Born correlations
open. For h>0 and Γ>0, the unique positive finite ground state follows Perron–Frobenius. Exact
matching-sector and degree-two counterexamples expose energetic preferences;
N=4/5 rings can be forced by graph feasibility, while N=6 admits disconnected
triangles at equal classical energy. The operator commutator has complete pair
support, and newly created direct links can yield cubic response across a long
snapshot distance for fresh tilted product matter and initially absent direct links. These results have explicit state/limit conditions;
they are not universal impossibility theorems for quantum spacetime.

## Known theory, project contributions and retained failures

Random-permutation longest-chain asymptotics, order-based dimension relations,
continuum causal reconstruction, ordinary-CSG restrictions, Ising response,
Perron–Frobenius symmetry and quantum-graphity pitfalls are established literature,
properly cited in the theory documents. Reproducing or independently deriving a
known result does not establish novelty.

The project's defensible contribution is a reusable collection of exact finite
witnesses, implementations, independently checked numerical records, adversarial
searches and scope distinctions. It produces original project artifacts, not a
substantiated claim of publication priority or a new physical law. Whether a
particular finite witness merits publication would require a dedicated literature
comparison; this report does not infer novelty from absence of a citation.

Failures remain part of the evidence. A pointwise equal-count fluctuation in
Experiment 1 did not reproduce in a separately planned, independently seeded post hoc follow-up. Experiment 3's failed
searches do not prove matched orders impossible. Experiment 4's nonsmooth-TV
bootstrap intervals can be biased upward; its postregistration full-vector
counterexample is labeled as such. Experiment 5 retains a failed smoke-stream
allocation, secondary reference rejections and finite-size bias. Experiment 6's supplied-chain reconstruction also deteriorates at h=0.5 as size
grows. Its nominal 95% bootstrap covered only 365/400 calibration means, 91.25%; replay did not
repair that coverage. The final study uses exact binomial bounds with whole
preparations as independent units, rather than silently upgrading the old intervals.


## Final experiment: useful effects, a failed advancement claim

Experiment 7 did not reduce to empty dynamics or independent random links. Born
laws differ from marginal-product and degree-matched nulls; link measurement changes
subsequent probabilities. At N=4, the six exact nonselective measurement comparisons
have future-law TV shifts from 0.131349 to 0.302554. Some Γ perturbations preserve
response rankings well. Those favorable effects coexist with the failed joint
benchmark: only 5/144 E preparations satisfy the connectedness condition across all
late ages, none meets both late-law stability limits, and no N=5 preparation passes
the concentration condition. Direct couplings, energetic preferences and supplied
initial asymmetry remain essential explanations.

The positive static chain control shows the response assay can distinguish nearby
and distant targets when locality is supplied. Complete and uncoupled controls
behave as predicted. Symmetric preparations retain equal labeled responses to
floating-point accuracy. The independent graph and state/statistical audits use
separate implementations; the complete frozen replay reproduces all 238 scientific artifacts byte for byte.
That tests arithmetic reproducibility, not new physical evidence. All raw records, uncertainty and failures are preserved in
the [final results](DYNAMICAL_LOCALITY_RESULTS.md).

Resource decisions were made before primary data: rejected complete timing pilots,
an exact frozen-sector numerical optimization, and openly committed planning
amendments 20→30→40 minutes. The final choice uses the worst retained timings,
not the fastest run, keeps the original 45-minute hard cap and 2 GiB memory cap, and
executes all cells at N=4/5 with 12 independent E preparations per cell. N=6 quantum
execution was excluded by a censored timing rule. This cohort cannot certify a
positive advancement even if all preparations pass; it can reject the observed
large failure. Limited precision is not evidence of universal impossibility.

A further exact pre-primary audit exposed bias in the extra N=5 GO gate. For the
degree null, connectivity gain equals
`[P(P5)−6P(C3 disjoint union K2)]/7`; requiring 0.1 forces P(P5)≥0.7. That is a
path-selective tiny-size condition, not a neutral definition of geometry. The
primary C/L/T/D NO-GO does not depend on that extra gate. The final decision therefore
preserves both a failed registered hypothesis and a limitation of its more ambitious
positive interpretation.

Graph-conditioned fresh-matter response can be concentrated even when the
unconditioned labeled average is uniform. Access to a measurement record changes
the operational question. Our unconditioned test does not exclude every relationally
addressed instrument, decohered branch or projected low-energy state family; none
has been shown here to yield a controlled physical continuum. This distinction
is a reason to state the boundary of the result, not to invent another simulation.

## Why causal relationships can encode geometry, and what was missing here

Within a suitable continuum spacetime class, causal ordering can constrain the
conformal metric; a compatible volume measure fixes its remaining scale. The
regularity, causality and measure assumptions matter. That is a reconstruction
statement about structures already satisfying geometric conditions, not a proof
that arbitrary relations or finite feature lists create a manifold. Experiment 4's
conditional interval result is a finite sampling-law illustration of this
conditional information, while its counterexamples show what selected summaries
lose.

A dynamical explanation must additionally derive why physically relevant states
and relational observables fall into such a class. Our classical growth and quantum
models inserted specific stochastic or Hamiltonian laws; none was derived from
an independently supported physical principle. Fixed subsystem factorization,
incidence, energetic preferences, measurement choices and external time remained
inputs. Knowing how information could encode geometry does not explain why nature
would select the required structures.

## What is excluded, what remains open, and a stopping rule

Exact counterexamples refute sufficiency of the selected finite summaries.
The six detectors and fixed-interaction MI models fail their registered advancement
criteria; this does not exclude every geometric interpretation. The fixed-p/ordinary-CSG
geometric sampling route is mathematically excluded only under the stated theorem
assumptions and convergence definition. Experiment 7 also establishes exact finite labeled-neighborhood obstructions
under permutation symmetry. The final experiment's NO-GO has its tested parameter, state and time scope;
it is not a phase diagram or a universal impossibility theorem.

Other microscopic laws, thermodynamic symmetry breaking, relational structure in
symmetric quantum states and controlled low-energy effective locality remain
logically open. The full operator's complete interaction support does not by
itself rule out an approximately local restricted sector with quantified leakage.
An unobserved target-only assay is also different from a relational instrument
with access to a graph record. Those possibilities have not been established here.
Their logical openness supplies no evidence that the present model realizes them.

A controlled continuum claim would need a justified sequence of states and laws,
normalization as size grows, physical subsystem/relational observables, controlled
coarse-graining, concentration or tightness, nondegenerate approximately unique
geometry, and separation of microscopic and macroscopic scales. Effective
propagation bounds must remain controlled on those scales, including leakage from
any proposed low-energy sector. Lorentzian causal structure, approximate Lorentz
symmetry, a physical matter sector and empirical units are additional obligations.
Einstein dynamics would require an effective gravitational law and appropriate
constraints/conservation/backreaction; a graph metric supplies none of these.

No experiment measures physical spacetime constituents, derives Einstein's
equations, establishes emergent time, models an astrophysical black-hole interior,
or resolves a gravitational singularity. A finite numerical state space having
no divergent stored number is not singularity resolution.

A meaningful restart would require an independently motivated physical principle,
a mathematical construction that addresses these controlled-limit conditions, or
a quantitative empirical prediction that discriminates the proposal from existing
physics. Another attractive graph, successful classifier, extra fitted term or
small-system dimension estimate is insufficient. This programme does not propose
Experiment 8 or another speculative Hamiltonian.
