## Statistical definitions preserved

- Experiments 1–4 primarily report **pointwise** intervals. They are not universal
  simultaneous confidence bands and do not quantify model misspecification.
- Experiment 1 controls reuse event counts; they are paired, not new independent
  physical draws. The post hoc follow-up is a separate diagnostic.
- Experiment 2 whole orders are independent units. Pair indicators within an order
  are correlated. Mean inversions differ from an inverse of the mean.
- Experiment 3 per-fit Wilson bounds condition on their fit. Hierarchical bootstrap
  resamples fits and whole orders, maintaining detector/search-stage pairing.
  Observed fit ranges are not confidence intervals.
- Experiment 4 conditional estimates are fitwise ratios of pooled counts, averaged
  equally across fits. They are not mean per-parent ratios. Overlapping intervals
  are not independent observations.
- Experiment 5 prefixes and thinning replicas remain in the same history cluster.
  The registered primary screen has approximate simultaneous bands over 48 entries;
  secondary pointwise intervals are labeled separately. Reference-control screens
  have their own eight-entry bands. Seed batches are not resampled as populations.
- TV percentile intervals may have lower bounds above a point estimate. Do not
  silently recenter them or interpret them as validated exact-law tests.
- Missing interval support is unavailable, not zero, success, or agreement.
- Experiment 6 resamples whole realizations; fields, perturbations, scrambles and
  evolution snapshots remain dependent. Its failed bootstrap coverage is preserved.
  The supplied-chain success gate has a separate exact binomial calculation.
- Experiment 7's exact two-sided Clopper–Pearson bounds adjust for twelve primary
  parameter/size cells. Source-target pairs, times and all paired controls belong
  to one preparation. Born enumeration adds no independent preparation units.
- Experiment 7's sample size cannot certify positive advancement even with 12/12
  successes, but its observed 0/12 permits the registered rejection. Geometric
  scaling stays unresolved. The 12-unit design and N6 exclusion were fixed by
  timing-only resource selection before primary data.
- Secondary graph-conditioned F summaries are descriptive and retain undefined
  mass. They neither replace the primary unconditioned estimand nor add a new
  confidence or equivalence result.

## Scope of the quantum findings and programme closure

Experiment 6 reconstructs relationships already supplied by fixed interactions.
Its descriptive proximity findings are useful; failed advancement criteria and
exact counterexamples prevent treating them as evidence for physical geometry.
The homogeneous finite ground state's symmetry statement assumes h>0; geometric
claims about arbitrary states or thermodynamic limits do not follow.

Experiment 7 rejects one registered finite unconditioned locality benchmark for
one Hamiltonian, six settings, N4/5, specified preparations, ages, delays and a
Pauli-Y intervention. It does not exclude all states or larger-size limits of that
Hamiltonian, all relational instruments, or all quantum-gravity mechanisms. At N5
the extra degree-null GO condition is path-selective: a connectivity advantage
of 0.1 requires P(P5) ≥ 0.7. That pre-primary audit limitation is visible; the primary
C/L/T/D NO-GO does not rely on the additional gate.

The final synthesis chooses **B: useful mathematical/computational research, but
insufficient evidence to pursue the physical hypothesis through more simulations**.
The programme is closed. Experiment 5's transition to quantum information and
Experiment 6's transition to dynamical links are historical, not invitations to
continue tuning. No Experiment 8 or replacement Hamiltonian is proposed. A restart
requires an independently justified physical principle, a controlled mathematical
construction or a discriminating empirical prediction.

## Scope of the Experiment 5 no-go

The broader restriction is for the nonnegative-coupling classical sequential-growth
class with t₀ or t₁ positive in Brightwell and Georgiou, under their expected
finite uniformly sampled labeled-suborder convergence. Their approximation by
semiorders in expected o(N²) pair-edit distance forces induced 2+2 and 3+1 densities
to vanish; a nondegenerate Minkowski diamond in d≥2 has positive 2+2 probability.
This does not cover every altered dynamics, quantum model, local scaling limit or
Lorentzian metric limit. Fixed-p chain-sampling convergence also does not imply
that the infinite order is total or that H/N→1.
